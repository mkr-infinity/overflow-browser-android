# Privacy Policy for Overflow Browser

Last updated: October 2026

Overflow Browser is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. We believe that what you browse on your device should stay on your device.

## Core Privacy Guarantees

1. **No Data Collection**: Overflow Browser does not collect, track, log, or sell any personal data, browsing history, download records, or search queries.
2. **Zero Analytics & Telemetry**: There are no third-party SDKs, crash trackers, or analytics libraries bundled into the application.
3. **Local-Only Storage**: All your data—bookmarks, history, open sessions, and custom settings—is stored locally in your device's private sandbox storage.
4. **Incognito / Private Tabs**: In Incognito mode:
   - Visited URLs are never written to the history log.
   - Tabs and form data are discarded when the tab is closed.
   - WebView cache and DOM storage are cleared upon closing all incognito tabs.
5. **Search Query Privacy**: When you type a search term, suggestions are fetched directly from your selected search provider (such as DuckDuckGo or Google) via HTTPS only if search suggestions are enabled in your settings. You can disable search suggestions at any time in Settings.
6. **Network Permissions**: Overflow Browser requests internet permissions strictly to load the websites you choose to visit. It requires no location, microphone, or camera access unless you explicitly grant permission to a website requesting media access (e.g., WebRTC video calls).

## Open Source

Overflow Browser is fully open source under the Apache 2.0 license. Anyone can inspect and audit the source code to verify these privacy commitments.
